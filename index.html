Setup · about a minute

Three steps, then you're driving

Install the small server on the computer you want to control. Everything else happens on your phone.

01

Run the server

Download and open DeskPad on your Windows PC. It sits in the system tray and shows a control panel with a pairing code.

02

Scan the QR

Open the DeskPad app, tap Scan QR, and point it at the code on your screen. The phone gets a key over the camera — never the network.

03

Control from anywhere

Trackpad, keyboard, media keys and the live screen viewer are now in your hand. This phone is remembered — no scanning next time.

Pair a phone
Pairing & security

The key travels by light, not by network

A typed PIN is short enough to guess. DeskPad shows a QR that carries a full 256-bit key — your phone reads it through the camera, so it never crosses the air where anyone could capture it.

  • End-to-end encrypted

    Every keystroke and frame is sealed with authenticated encryption. Even on shared Wi-Fi, the traffic is unreadable.

  • Paired once, trusted after

    After the first scan your phone holds a private token. Reconnecting is instant — and you can revoke any device from the control panel.

  • Stays on your network

    No relay servers, no accounts, no telemetry. Phone and computer talk directly on the same Wi-Fi and nowhere else.
